# Privacy Policy

**InnerCircle** ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## Information we collect

### Account information
- Email address and display name (provided when you register)
- Date of birth (optional)
- Password (stored only as a one-way hash; never in plain text)

### Conversation content
- Messages you send to AI companions
- Memories extracted from conversations (facts the app chooses to remember)
- Custom personas you create (name, personality description, avatar)

### Technical information
- Device and app version identifiers used for push notifications
- Approximate interaction timestamps for rate limiting and abuse prevention

### What we do not collect
- Payment card data (billing is not integrated yet)
- Contacts, precise GPS location, or microphone/camera access beyond optional photo upload for your avatar

## How we use your information
- To provide chat, memory, and persona features
- To authenticate you and keep your account secure (login rate limiting, password reset)
- To send check-in reminders you explicitly schedule
- To respond to support requests

## Third-party services
- **Groq** — processes chat text to generate AI replies
- **Google Firebase** — cloud messaging for optional push notifications
- **PostgreSQL host** — stores app data (see your deployment/hosting provider)

Each third party processes data under its own terms.

## Data retention
- Account data is kept until you delete your account
- Deleting your account removes conversations, memories, and profile data associated with your user ID
- Backups may retain deleted data for a limited window managed by the database host
